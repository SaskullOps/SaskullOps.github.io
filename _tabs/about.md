Hi, I'm **Alex Guardiola**.

This is my learning notebook. I work in IT, coming from logistics operations, and I use this site to build in public the things I want to understand better: automation, my homelab, security and where AI actually helps.

### What I'm learning by building

- **Automation and reporting at work.** Power Platform, SQL, Power BI and M365, with Python and n8n where they fit better. Lately I'm testing where AI assistance saves time in reporting and where a plain script does the job.
- **A product, end to end.** QRFleet is the fleet damage reporting app I built for a logistics company. It runs in production and taught me more about deployment, monitoring and mistakes than any course.
- **A homelab that runs itself.** A Raspberry Pi with around 18 services, reachable only over a VPN, with backups, alerts and updates handled automatically.
- **Security from the defensive side.** A small SOC at home with Wazuh and CrowdSec, plus CTF rooms to keep the offensive side in view.

### How to read this site

Posts explain what I built and why I made each decision, including what went wrong. [Projects](/projects/) collects the bigger pieces. Nothing here is polished advice: it is a record of what I tried and what I learned.
