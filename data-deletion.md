To delete any data the @s4skull Instagram automation may hold about you (the id of a comment you wrote and its processing timestamp), use either option:

- Send a direct message to [@s4skull](https://www.instagram.com/s4skull/) on Instagram with the text `DELETE MY DATA`.
- Open an issue at [github.com/SaskullOps/SaskullOps.github.io/issues](https://github.com/SaskullOps/SaskullOps.github.io/issues) with your Instagram username (don't include anything else).

Deletion is done within 30 days and confirmed through the same channel. Deleting your comment on Instagram also stops any further processing.
